01 Service · Airport transfers

Airport runs that watch the flight, not the clock.

JFK, LGA, Newark, Teterboro, Westchester. We pull live FAA data on every booked flight, re-cut the pickup window when wheels-up slips, and let the chauffeur know before the rider does.

  • 01 Flight tracking is on by default. FlightAware feed posts deltas to the assigned trip every two minutes. No more "is the flight on time?" calls at 3 a.m.
  • 02 Curbside swap on a tap. If a rider lands at a different terminal than booked, they switch the meet point in the app and dispatch sees it before the chauffeur pulls up to the wrong door.
  • 03 Wait time, not surge time. First fifteen minutes after wheels-down are free. Beyond that, $1.20/min — never surged, never surprised.
  • 04 Frequent-flyer profiles. We remember Cynthia takes a sedan, not an SUV. We remember Mr. Patel won't ride without bottled water. The rider doesn't have to keep telling us.

02 Service · Long-distance

Long-distance built around the chauffeur's hours, not just yours.

NYC ↔ Boston, NYC ↔ DC, NYC ↔ Philadelphia, NYC ↔ Hamptons, NYC ↔ Burlington. Trips longer than two hours come with mandated rest stops, a clean back seat, and — on overnight legs — a second driver waiting at the relay.

  • 01 Pre-quoted, dollar-fixed. We price every long-distance leg by the mile, plus tolls at cost. No "the meter said". You see the number before you book it.
  • 02 Rotating drivers on overnights. Anything past 4 hours one-way and we plan a relay. The car keeps moving. The chauffeur isn't dangerous.
  • 03 Wifi, water, charger, not optional. Every long-distance vehicle is set up before it leaves the depot — confirmed by the driver's pre-shift checklist.
  • 04 Dog-friendly on request. We have three vehicles in rotation that take dogs. Tell us when you book; the cabin gets the right blankets and a different chauffeur.

03 Service · Proms & events

The prom, the wedding, the milestone — without the group-chat panic.

14-passenger stretch limousines, multi-stop manifests, and a chauffeur who's done senior nights for eight years. Parents see live ETAs. Photographers see arrival times. Nobody has to text anyone privately.

  • 01 Multi-pickup manifests. Up to twelve pickup addresses on a single trip. Each parent gets a live ETA — they can stop refreshing the family group chat.
  • 02 Chaperone-friendly. Drivers are background-checked, alcohol policy is no-tolerance, and we report back to the booking parent — not just the rider — at the end of the night.
  • 03 The interior the kids actually want. Cool blue floor lights. Bluetooth audio that survives a senior class. A bar with sparkling water and ice — no minibar of secrets.
  • 04 Wedding day producer mode. On weddings, we assign a single dispatcher to your timeline. They're on the phone with the photographer, the venue, and the maid of honor — so the bride isn't.

04 Service · Corporate & executive

Corporate accounts that don't need a phone call to book a ride.

White-labelled portal for travel desks, monthly invoicing with cost-center splits, dedicated chauffeurs for principals, and a single roadshow manifest that everyone in the convoy can read.

  • 01 Dedicated travel-desk login. Your assistant books on the portal, sees live availability, and never has to call us. We still answer if they want.
  • 02 Net-30 invoicing, signed off. One monthly statement. Cost-center splits. PO numbers. The columns your finance team has already decided they need.
  • 03 Roadshow manifests, not 14 separate trips. We move investor roadshows as one orchestrated convoy — five stops, three vehicles, one dispatcher on the line. Nobody loses their schedule.
  • 04 Principal-level chauffeurs. For C-suite and family principals: the same chauffeur every time, on retainer. They learn the building, the gate code, and how loud the music's allowed to be.
  • 05 SOC 2 Type II. Travel data is sensitive. We treat it that way — encrypted at rest, audited annually, deletable on request.
