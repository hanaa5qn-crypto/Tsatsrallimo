Issue 04 · Cover essay

The dispatcher who finally slept through the night.

For seven years our father took the 4 a.m. JFK call himself. Then we wrote a piece of software that knew which car was where, and one Tuesday in October he didn't pick up — because nothing was wrong.

This issue is about that Tuesday. About what it took to teach the night shift that the answer to "where's the car?" can come from a screen instead of a phone call. About why "boring at scale" is the hardest design constraint we know.
